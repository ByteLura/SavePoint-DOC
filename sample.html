The Coin Collector sample

The shipped sample is a complete mini game loop wired to SavePoint Pro end to end — not a code snippet.

  • Slot select on the product's own slot screen: pick a slot, start a new game.
  • Play: collect coins with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Autosave every 20 seconds via the SavePointAutosave component, plus a Checkpoint button that writes a named checkpoint slot.
  • Relaunch and Load: stop Play, press Play again, load the slot — coins, position, and playtime are restored.

Import it from the home window's Open Samples button (Package Manager > SavePoint Pro > Samples > Coin Collector also works). After playing, open the home window's Saves tab: the slot you made is listed with its real metadata — summary, playtime, data version.

The sample is the fastest way to see every headline feature working before you wire your own game: slots, autosave, checkpoints, and a restored run after a relaunch.
